The type case

Presses per letter from everyone who has typed here, and what that has done to each sort. Capitals and accented letters share their base letter's wear, as on a keyboard.

How the wear works

Each dot in a glyph has its own breaking point, set by a seeded random number and by how exposed it is: dots on the outer edge and in the joins break first. A dot shrinks before it drops out. Wear for a letter follows presses ÷ (presses + 100).

Everyone, or just you

Everyone shows the shared font: real keystrokes from every visitor, summed. Just you is your own case, kept in this browser, where you can also add simulated years of English or Dutch. Dutch hits e much harder (about 19% of letters) and wears n, v, j and z far faster than English.

Prior art

Memoire (Michelle Ando and Ryan Bugden, 2016) wore down across a printed issue, from drawn start and end states. Koruption and Hel have destruction axes. Lowie Worn differs in one way: each letter wears from its own use, live.

About Lowie

A connected hand-poke serif, open source under the SIL OFL. GitHub · specimen. The joins here are Lowie's own: high from b, o, r, v, w, low from the rest.
