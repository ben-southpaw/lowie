Where wear comes from

Sorts

A metal letter wears each time it meets paper. The letters set most wore out first, and printers bought more of them. Edges and fine joins went before anything else.

Keys

A keyboard keeps a record of its owner. The e, the a and the space bar go shiny first. Nobody chooses the pattern. The language does.

Skin

Hand-poked ink spreads and fades as skin renews. Loose dots go first and dense patches last longest. Lowie is drawn from those dots, so it wears the same way.

Related work: Memoire by Michelle Ando and Ryan Bugden (2016) degraded across a printed issue from drawn start and end states. Koruption and Hel erode along a variable axis. Lowie Worn wears each letter from its own use, live, and shared.
